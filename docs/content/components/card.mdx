### Link card

Give a `Card` an `href` and the whole card becomes one link (an `<a>`), the same way `Button` does. A link card takes a `title` (required), an optional `description` and a trailing `hint` (a reading time, "Learn more →"); children render between the description and the hint. Keep link cards free of other interactive content: a button or link inside a link is invalid.

<Demo id="card.link" />

### Description, title badge and pressable cards

Every card takes `description`, and `titleEnd` for content at the end of the title row, such as a
status `Badge`. In Astro that's the `title-end` slot. Link cards accept a node `title`.

`onClick` makes a div card pressable: the title becomes a `<button>` whose hit area covers the
card. The card gets button semantics, focus and Enter/Space, with the title as its name, and no
block content ends up inside a button. Links and buttons inside the card keep working.

```tsx
<Card
  title="Memory"
  titleEnd={<Badge tone="success">Saved</Badge>}
  description="Tuesday"
  onClick={open}
/>
```
