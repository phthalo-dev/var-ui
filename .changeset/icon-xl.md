`IconButton size="xl"` carries a matching 24px glyph. New `size.icon.xl` token (1.5rem) and `Icon size="xl"`; `IconButton` no longer maps `xl` to the `lg` glyph. With `pill` it is a circle, for primary play-style actions: `<IconButton size="xl" pill tone="accent" appearance="solid" icon={…} aria-label="Play" />`.
