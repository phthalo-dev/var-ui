`Button` and `IconButton` take `size="xs"`, a 20px button for dense rows, tags and code-block headers. An invisible layer grows its pointer target to 24px (WCAG 2.2 target size) without changing layout. New `size.icon.xs` token (12px) and `Icon size="xs"`. `xs` is button-only: text inputs and selects keep the `sm`–`lg` control scale.
