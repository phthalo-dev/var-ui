The `textBlock` recipe (React/Astro `Text`) gains an `eyebrow` variant (uppercase with tracking for label treatments), a `whiteSpace` axis (`pre-wrap` preserves newlines and wrapping), an `xl` size, a `bold` weight, and an `n` line-clamp mode driven by the new `--var-ui-text-block-lineclamp` integer variable for arbitrary line counts.
